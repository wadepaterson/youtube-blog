Speaker Accelerator: How to Transform Your Public Speaking Skills in Just Three Weeks

By Wade Paterson

How would your life change if you could become a confident, compelling public speaker? Think about the ripple effects — on your career, your relationships, and the causes you care about most. For many professionals, the gap between where they are and where they want to be on stage (or on camera) feels enormous. But what if you could close that gap in just three weeks? That's the promise behind Wade Paterson's Speaker Accelerator, an immersive virtual program designed to help executives, leaders, and professionals rapidly sharpen their communication skills.

Why the Speaker Accelerator Exists

Public speaking is consistently ranked as the most common personal fear in the world — yet it's also one of the most valuable professional skills anyone can develop. Programs like Toastmasters offer tremendous long-term growth, but not everyone has the time, capacity, or energy to commit to a regular club schedule. The Speaker Accelerator was created to fill that gap: a focused, six-session virtual course that delivers meaningful results on an accelerated timeline.

Launched in July 2025, the program has been refined through multiple iterations, with each cohort helping to make the experience sharper, more practical, and more impactful than the last.

What You'll Learn Across Six Sessions

The Speaker Accelerator covers the essential pillars of effective public speaking, broken into six structured virtual sessions over three weeks:

  • Session 1 — Speech Structure: Learn how to hook an audience's attention from the very first sentence and craft a speech that comes full circle, tying everything together with purpose and polish.
  • Session 2 — Humor, Nerves, and Filler Words: Discover how to use humor as one of the most powerful tools in a speaker's toolkit. Tackle the challenge of overcoming nerves and anxiety so you feel genuinely confident on stage or in front of a camera. Plus, learn strategies to eliminate those pesky filler words — the ums, uhs, and ahs — that quietly undermine your credibility.
  • Session 3 — Body Language: Explore how what you do with your body and facial expressions often matters more than the words you speak. This session dives deep into the nonverbal side of communication.
  • Session 4 — Vocal Variety and Video Content: Master the art of using your voice — getting louder for emphasis, slowing your pace to let a point land, or speeding up to pull your audience deeper into a story. This session also covers video content creation, along with a special surprise for participants.
  • Session 5 — Advanced Topics: Go beyond the fundamentals with guidance on leveraging AI tools for speech preparation, sitting on panels, and handling questions during podcast interviews.
  • Session 6 — Final Speech Day: Present the speech you've been building throughout the program to your fellow accelerator participants. This is where everything comes together.

You Leave with a Tangible Result

One of the most distinctive features of the Speaker Accelerator is that it isn't just theory. Throughout the program, you work on your own speech — refining it session by session. By the final day, you deliver a polished five-to-seven-minute presentation to your cohort. That means you walk away from the program with a real, ready-to-use speech that you can deploy in your professional life, whether it's a keynote, a team meeting, a conference talk, or any other setting where your voice matters.

Who Is It For?

The Speaker Accelerator is ideal for executives, leaders, and professionals who want to become more effective communicators but need a structured, time-efficient path to get there. If you've always known that improving your public speaking could unlock new opportunities — but haven't found the right format to make it happen — this program was designed with you in mind.
